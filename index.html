Sanctuary Guide

How Your Journal Works

Sanctuary is a quiet space designed to help you unload everything on your mind without clutter or friction.

1. Ephemeral by Default (Or Save When Wanted)

By default, your journal is Not Saving—closing or refreshing the tab erases every word for complete privacy. If you wish to keep your entry, click the save status pill at the bottom to enable Local Storage or Cloud Sync.

2. Release & Let Go Ritual

When you finish writing out your thoughts and want emotional closure, click Release & Let Go to watch your words dissolve into floating mist.

3. In-Page Prompts & Ambient Sounds

Click Prompt in the top bar to reveal a guiding question right below today's date inside your page. Use the Sounds menu to play Soft Rain, Forest Wind, Singing Bowl, or Ocean Waves while you write.

4. Quick Wipe Shortcut
Instantly clear the page without animation
Press Esc twice
